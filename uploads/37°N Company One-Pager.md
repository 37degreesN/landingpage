## Mission: End Generic Health

One-size-fits-all health advice is a relic of the past. Traditional medicine relies on statistical averages that ignore individual biochemistry, while the wellness industry drowns users in generic, unsustainable routines. 37 Degrees North (37°N) is building Billion Heartbeats to end generic health. By mapping personalized health protocols to first-principles cellular science—understanding exactly why an intervention works at the protein and cellular level—we empower individuals to transition from passive patients to active, data-driven creators of their own wellness journeys.

## The Problem

Modern health technology is bottlenecked by clinical trials that only show population-level averages, leaving individuals guessing what actually works for their unique biology. Furthermore, consumers face a massive mental blocker when sharing personal health data, while developers get trapped trying to build overcomplicated "mega-apps" that suffer from low engagement. The industry has lost sight of what matters: simple, actionable, and scientifically validated personal experimentation.

## The Product: Billion Heartbeats

Billion Heartbeats is an AI-native health platform structured around a clean, elegant loop: Insights, Experiments, and Protocols. Instead of building a single, heavy application, our core engine powers a suite of modular health skills and "tiny, disposable microapps." 

Key product pillars include:  
\- First-Principles Protocols: An "AlphaFold for protocols" that bypasses slow clinical trial bottlenecks by mapping behavioral interventions directly to cellular-level mechanisms.  
\- Microapps as Display Ads: Simple, single-purpose tools designed for rapid generation, testing, and sharing. These microapps leverage product-led growth (PLG) mechanics—they are simple to use and make the user look good when shared, eliminating the friction of traditional health tracking.  
\- Hybrid Privacy & Persistence: A secure data model inspired by HIPAA-compliant systems like Epic, combined with seamless, Zep-like data persistence to make tracking daily habits effortless.
